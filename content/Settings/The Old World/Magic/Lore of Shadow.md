# Attack
**Concentration:** Yes
**Skills:** Arcana, Divine, Primal
Magic attacks are combat checks, and they follow the normal rules for performing a combat check, on page 101, using the character’s magic skill instead of a combat skill. There are some exceptions, however, which we detail here. When making a magic attack, the character must select one target at short range (but not engaged). The default difficulty of the check is **Easy ( #GenesysDifficulty )**. The attack deals damage equal to the characteristic linked to the skill used to make the attack (so if the character uses Arcana, they would deal damage equal to their Intellect), plus 1 damage per uncanceled #GenesysSuccess . The attack has no set Critical rating, so you may only inflict a Critical Injury with a #GenesysTriumph .

# Augment
**Concentration:** Yes
**Skills:** Divine, Primal
This is using magic to enhance people. A character selects one target they are engaged with (which can be themself), then makes a Primal or Divine skill check. The default difficulty of the check is **Average ( #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty )**. If the check is successful, until the end of your character’s next turn, the target increases the ability of any skill checks they make by one (in effect, this means they add #GenesysAbility to their checks). A character may not be affected by more than one Augment spell at the same time (so no stacking effects).

# Barrier
**Concentration:** Yes
**Skills:** Arcana, Divine
Both arcane and divine spellcasters have the power to create barriers of magical energy to protect themselves and their allies. The character selects one target they are engaged with (which can be themself), then makes an Arcana or Divine skill check. The default difficulty of
the check is **Easy ( #GenesysDifficulty )**. If the check is successful, until the end of the character’s next turn, reduce the damage of all hits the target suffers by one, and further reduce it by one for every uncanceled #GenesysSuccess #GenesysSuccess beyond the first.

# Curse
**Concentration:** Yes
**Skills:** Arcana, Divine
This action represents the combat use of curse magic. Your character selects one target within short range, then makes an Arcana or Divine skill check. The default difficulty of the check is **Average ( #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty )**. If it is successful, until the end of the character’s next turn, the target decreases the ability of any skill checks they make by one (in effect, this means they remove one #GenesysAbility from their checks).

# Dispel
**Concentration:** No
**Skills:** Arcana
The ability to nullify magic is a strange and wondrous art that only certain arcane spellcasters possess. The character selects one target within short range that is under the effects of a spell, then makes an Arcana skill check. The default difficulty for the check is **Hard ( #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty )**. If the check is successful, the effects the target is under immediately end (if the spell affected multiple targets, the other targets remain affected).

# Mask
**Concentration:** Yes
**Skills:** Arcana
This is using magic to create an illusion, or to disguise a character’s appearance. The character makes an Arcana skill check. The default difficulty for the check is Easy ( #GenesysDifficulty ). If the check is successful, the character creates an illusion of a creature or object that is silhouette 1 or smaller. The illusion appears within short range of the character. Alternatively, the illusion changes the appearance of the caster or one silhouette 1 (or smaller) target they are engaged with. The illusion cannot obscure the basic size and shape of the target.

Illusions can generate light and sound, but they cannot cause harm or interact with their environment in any way. Illusions can be animated and can move, as long as they remain within the range of the spell. A keen observer can attempt to spot the false nature of the illusion by making an **Average ( #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty ) Vigilance check** (or **Average ( #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty ) Perception check** if the observer suspects that their senses may be fooled). Success means the observer recognizes the object’s illusory nature.

# Sense Magic
**Skills:** Arcana
Magical sense, commonly known as Witchsight is the ability of most spellcasters to sense the presence or absence of magic. All people born with the talent to work magic have some
degree of witchsight that can be developed and expanded with training, so the spellcaster
can actually see the ebb and flow of the Winds of Magic, witness the scintillating colors of the
Chaos energy in its raw form, and be better able to harness it for the casting of spells.

When using Sense Magic narratively, it has a passive effect. Your character constantly sees
the world as if through two types of vision superimposed upon each other. With their normal mundane sight, they see like any other person does. With their witchsight, the character actually sees the Winds of Magic to some degree, either as swirling clouds and rivers of churning colored energy, or as manifestations of their own thoughts, memories, emotions, expectations, beliefs, and fears and those of the people around them. When your character tries to identify the exact composition of the Winds of Magic or recognize magic objects or creatures, it requires a check with basic difficulty of Easy ( #GenesysDifficulty). Identifying magic items or trying to trace another magic user may increase the difficulty to Hard ( #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty).
