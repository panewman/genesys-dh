# Psychic Mastery
**Tier:** 5  
**Activation:** Active-maneuver  
**Ranked:** No  
Only Psykers may benefit from this talent. Once per encounter, your character may perform a psychic power check as a maneuver. This can be used to allow them to perform two psychic power checks in one turn.
