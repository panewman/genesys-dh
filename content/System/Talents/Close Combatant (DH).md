# Close Combatant
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
When you purchase this talent, your character gains Melee (Light) or Melee (Heavy) as a career skill. In addition, once per session your character may use this talent to remove #GenesysSetback from a melee combat check they make.
