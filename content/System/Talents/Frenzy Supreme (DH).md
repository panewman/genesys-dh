# Frenzy (Supreme)
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-maneuver  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Frenzy (Improved) talent to benefit from this talent. While under the effects of Frenzy, your character can make ranged combat checks during their turn as long as they have spent at least one maneuver moving towards a potential melee target this turn. In addition, they can attempt to break their frenzy as a maneuver rather than an action, once per turn.
