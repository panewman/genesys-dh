# Bloodhound
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
When your character makes a check to follow another character’s trail (whether their physical tracks or a trail of information), you may use this talent to add #GenesysSuccess no greater than your character’s ranks in Streetwise or Survival, as appropriate to the environment, to the check results.
