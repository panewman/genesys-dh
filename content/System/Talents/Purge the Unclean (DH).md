# Purge the Unclean
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-action  
**Ranked:** No  
As an action, you may spend a Story Point to intone holy words and repel a warp entity within short range. Make an opposed Discipline vs Discipline check against the warp entity. If the check succeeds, the creature is repelled, unable to approach to engaged range until the end of their next turn. #GenesysAdvantage can be spent to inflict 1 wound on the entity, ignoring soak (this can be done multiple times). #GenesysThreat may be spent to inflict 1 strain on your character (this can also be done multiple times).
