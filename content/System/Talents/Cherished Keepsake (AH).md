# Cherished Keepsake
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character has a small item that is of great affection to them (such as a stuffed animal, a rabbit’s foot, a lucky dice, or a favorite whiskey bottle). While that item is in your character’s possession, they gain b on all fear checks they make. If you character loses the item, they lose the benefit of this talent until they replace the item with another one (most likely in between game sessions).
