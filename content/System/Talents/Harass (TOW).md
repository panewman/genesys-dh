# Harass
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Small, But Vicious Dog or Animal Companion talent to benefit from this talent.  
Whenever the character's animal companion makes a successful combat check against a target, it may forgo inflicting damage to upsrade the difficulty of the target's next check once instead.
