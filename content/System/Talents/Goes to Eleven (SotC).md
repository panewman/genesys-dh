# Goes to Eleven
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** Yes  
Your character increases the range of their musical talents by one range band per rank of Goes to Eleven. A musical talent is any talent that requires your character to "use a musical instrument" to activate it. Narratively, you should explain how your character augmented or amplified their instrument.
