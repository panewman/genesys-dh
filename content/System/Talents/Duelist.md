# Duelist
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character adds #GenesysBoost to their melee combat checks while engaged with a single opponent. Your character adds #GenesysSetback to their melee combat checks while engaged with three or more opponents.
