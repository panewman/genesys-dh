# Daring Aviator
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** Yes  
Before your character makes a Driving or Piloting check, they may add a number of #GenesysThreat to the results to use this talent to add an equal number of #GenesysSuccess . The number may not exceed your character’s ranks in Daring Aviator.
