# Dramatic Flourish
**Tier:** 4  
**Activation:** Active-maneuver  
**Ranked:** No  
Once per round, a character may suffer 2 Strain, upgrading the difficulty of the next Melee (Light) or Melee (Heavy) combat check they make once. If the check succeeds, the character adds #GenesysTriumph to the results; if it fails, #GenesysDespair .
