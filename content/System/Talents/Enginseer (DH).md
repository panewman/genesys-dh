# Enginseer
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
When you purchase this talent, your character gains Driving or Tech-Use as a career skill. In addition, once per session your character may use this talent to remove any number of #GenesysSetback imposed by using faulty or damaged equipment on a single skill check, so long as that equipment is still in a usable state.
