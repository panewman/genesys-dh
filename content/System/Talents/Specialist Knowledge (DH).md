# Specialist Knowledge
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** Yes  
When you gain this talent, select a specific area of expertise for your character. When making a Forbidden Lore or Scholastic Lore check which pertains to that area of expertise, reduce the difficulty of the check once. Each additional time you purchase this talent for your character, select two additional areas of expertise Some examples of appropriate areas of expertise include: Archaeotech, Cryptology, Imperial Creed, Legend, Numerology, Mutants, The Warp, Underworld.
