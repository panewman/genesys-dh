# Faerie Finesse
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character add #GenesysSuccess to the results of social skill checks they make targeting faeries and pixies and to modify or reprogram the same. In addition, when a faerie or pixie acting under your character’s instructions provides assistance, it adds one additional #GenesysBoost to the check.
