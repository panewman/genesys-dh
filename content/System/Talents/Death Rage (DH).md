# Death Rage
**Tier:** 4  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character adds +2 damage to melee attacks for each Critical Injury they are currently suffering. (Your GM may also impose additional penalties on social skill checks your character makes while enraged due to Critical Injuries they are suffering.)
