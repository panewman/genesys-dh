# Wallcrawler
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character can move across vertical surfaces as easily as horizontal surfaces. In addition, when your character attempts to reduce the damage taken from falling, reduce the difficulty of the Athletics or Coordination check by one.
