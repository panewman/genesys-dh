# Conditioned
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
When you purchase this talent, your character gains Athletics or Coordination as a career skill. In addition, your character ignores a single #GenesysSetback imposed on skill checks due to exhaustion, lack of sleep, hunger, or similar fatigue-induced penalties.
