# Weapon Training
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** Yes  
When you purchase this talent for your character, select one class of weapon, such as bolt weapons, las weapons, or chain weapons. Prior to making an attack check using a weapon of this type, your character may suffer 3 strain to add #GenesysSuccess #GenesysSuccess to the check’s results. Each additional time you purchase this talent for your character, select two additional weapon classes.
