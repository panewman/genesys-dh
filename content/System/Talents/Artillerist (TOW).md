# Artillerist
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Gunnery is now a career skill for your character. A number of allied characters within medium range may use your character's ranks in Gunnery instead of theirs when making Gunnery checks. The number cannot exceed your character's Presence rating.
