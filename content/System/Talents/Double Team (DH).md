# Double Team
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
When your character and an ally are both engaged in melee combat with the same enemy, add #GenesysBoost to all melee combat checks targeting that enemy. If your ally also has the Double Team talent, add #GenesysBoost #GenesysBoost instead.
