# Takedown
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-action  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character may use this talent to make an opposed Melee (Light) versus Resilience check targeting one engaged opponent. If the check succeeds, the target is knocked prone and is immobilized until the end of your character’s next turn. If the target is a minion or rival, your character can spend #GenesysTriumph to incapacitate (but not kill) the target instead.
