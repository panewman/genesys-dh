# Pain Don’t Hurt
**Tier:** 4  
**Activation:** Active-maneuver  
**Ranked:** No  
This talent costs 2 Æmber. Once per session, your character may consume 1 Æmber to use this talent to reduce all damage they suffer by one plus their ranks in Resilience until the end of the following round.
