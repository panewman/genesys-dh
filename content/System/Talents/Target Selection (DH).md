# Target Selection
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
When making a Ranged combat check targeting an opponent engaged with one of your character’s allies, downgrade the difficulty of the check once (thus negating the penalty for shooting at engaged targets).
