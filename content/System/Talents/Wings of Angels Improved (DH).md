# Wings of Angels (Improved)
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-maneuver  
**Ranked:** Yes  
Your character must have purchased the Wings of Angels talent to benefit from this talent. When using the Wings of Angels talent to perform a jump with your jump pack, your character may move to anywhere within long range of their current position.
