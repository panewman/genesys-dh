# Aegis of Contempt
**Tier:** 5  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Armour of Contempt talent to benefit from this talent. Whenever an ally within short range would gain Corruption, reduce the amount by 1, to a minimum of 1. They may spend a Story Point to instead reduce the amount by 4.
