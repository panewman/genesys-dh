# Chameleon
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
You cause reality to blur around you, distorting your image and allowing you to blend in with your surroundings. You may use the Telepathy or Biomancy skill in place of the Stealth skill when attempting to conceal yourself.
