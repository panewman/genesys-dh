# Weak Spot
**Tier:** 4  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
Before your character makes a ranged combat check, you may spend a Story Point to use this talent. While resolving the check, the weapon gains the Vicious item quality equal to your character's ranks in Firearms or Ranged skill.
