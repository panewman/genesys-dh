# Litany of Hate
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-action  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Hatred talent to benefit from this talent. Select a group for which you have the Hatred talent. As an action, make an Average ( #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty ) Leadership check. If successful, a number of targets within earshot equal to your Presence gain the Hatred talent targeting the same group. This effect lasts until the end of the encounter.
