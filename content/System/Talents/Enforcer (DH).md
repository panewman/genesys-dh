# Enforcer
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
When you purchase this talent, your character gains Coercion or Resilience as a career skill. In addition, once per session your character may use this talent to add #GenesysBoost to a melee combat check or social skill check targeting an opponent with a lower Brawn score than your character.
