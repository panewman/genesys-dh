# Undercity Contacts
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
Once per session, you may spend one to Story Point use this talent to let your character learn if a character of your choice is in New Angeles, and if so, what district.  
At your GM’s discretion, the information may take up to an hour to come back to your character.
