# Rule of Ghur
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
When your character casts an Attack spell, they may use this talent to add the Close Combat effect to the spell without increasing the difficulty.  
Purchasing this talent also has a narrative effect as it subtly changes the appearance of your character. Their hair grows thick and disheveled and their nails become long and sharpened.
