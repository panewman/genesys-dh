# Sword Master
**Tier:** 4  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
Once per encounter, after rolling a melee attack but before resolving the check, you may spend a Story Point to use this talent. While resolving the check, the weapon gains the Stun Damage item quality and the Stun item quality equal to your character's ranks in Discipline.
