# Power Through
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
When in an ongoing hostile or actively danserous scene or environment, where your character has received at least 1 wound in the previous encounter, your character may make a Simple (-) Resilience check, instead of Discipline or Cool, to recover strain at the end of an encounter (see page 117 of Genesys Core Rulebook). Note: Originally from The Forge, episode 6.
