# Hand on the Throttle
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
Once per round while operating a vehicle, your character may use this talent to increase or decrease its speed by 1, to a minimum of 0 or a maximum of the vehicle’s maximum speed.
