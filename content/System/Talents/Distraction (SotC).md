# Distraction!
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Active-action  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character may use this talent to make an Average ( #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty ) Coordination, Deception, or Skulduggery check. For each #GenesysSuccess the check generates, one adversary of your choice within short range becomes disoriented. Your character may spend #GenesysAdvantage from the check to inflict 1 strain on an affected adversary and may spend #GenesysTriumph from the check to stagger a minion or rival within short range.
