# Face of the Wild
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
When your character casts a transform spell on themself, you may spend a Story Point to have them use this talent to maintain the effects of the spell until the end of the encounter, without performing concentrate maneuvers.
