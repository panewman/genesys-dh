# Honed Senses
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** Yes  
When you gain this Talent, select one of the five senses. Add #GenesysBoost to all Perception and Vigilance checks involving this sense. Each additional time you purchase this talent for your character, select another sense.
