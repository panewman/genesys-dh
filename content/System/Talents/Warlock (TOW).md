# Warlock
**Tier:** 5  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
Increase damage of your character's Attack spells by 2 for each Dark Gift they have.  
Additionally, once per encounter, your character may suffer 1 corruption to use this talent. Until the end of the encounter, the character can cast spells without suffering strain and don't need to perform concentrate maneuvers to sustain their spells.
