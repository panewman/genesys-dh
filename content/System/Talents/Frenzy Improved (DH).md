# Frenzy (Improved)
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Active-action  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Frenzy talent to benefit from this talent. Your character can enter Frenzy as a maneuver. Additionally, as an action your character can attempt to end their Frenzy, making a Hard ( #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty ) Cool check. If successful, they break out of frenzy prematurely.
