# Unnatural Aim
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Active-action  
**Ranked:** No  
Suffer 2 strain to make an attack with one of your ranged weapons, substituting the usual attack skill for Divination. Regardless of your actual distance from the target, the attack is considered to be made from Short range, making the base difficulty Easy ( #GenesysChallenge ).
