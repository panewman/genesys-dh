# Quick Witted
**Tier:** 4  
**Activation:** Active-incidential-oot  
**Ranked:** No  
Once per encounter, after another character makes a social skull check, your character may use this talent to make an Average ( #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty ) Vigilance check. If successful, you may add a number of #GenesysSuccess or #GenesysFailure (your choice) equal to your character's ranks in Charm to the other character’s check. If your character fails, your character suffers 3 strain.
