# Call to Vengeance
**Tier:** 4  
**Activation:** Active-action  
**Ranked:** No  
This talent may only be activated once per session on your character’s first turn following another player character becoming incapacitated due to suffering excess wounds, or dying. As an action, your character can raise their weapon in defiance and speak an oath of hatred, or recite a canticle of vengeance. No matter how they express their rage, the effect is the same; your character, and all player characters within long range (except those who are incapacitated) remove all strain. At the GM’s discretion, particularly important allied NPCs who have formed a close bond with the group may be counted as player characters for the purpose of this talent.
