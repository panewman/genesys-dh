# Hunter’s Eye
**Tier:** 4  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
Once per encounter, your character may choose a visible target in Extreme range and suffer 2 Strain, reducing the Critical Rating of all Ranged (Light) and Ranged (Heavy) attacks they make against that target by 2, to a minimum of 1, for the rest of the encounter.
