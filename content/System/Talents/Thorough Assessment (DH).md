# Thorough Assessment
**Tier:** 4  
**Activation:** Active-action  
**Ranked:** No  
Once per session, your character may make a Hard ( #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty ) knowledge check (the GM and player should determine which knowledge skill is most applicable in the given situation) to perform the Thorough Assessment action. If you succeed, your character may add #GenesysBoost to any check another character makes before the end of the encounter, but only if that character could reasonably benefit from this information. For every #GenesysSuccess beyond the first, you may add #GenesysBoost to one additional check, however you cannot allocate more than #GenesysBoost to any single check this way. At the end of the encounter, any unused #GenesysBoost are lost.
