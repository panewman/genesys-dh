# Born in the Saddle
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
Once per encounter, your character may use this talent to add #GenesysSuccess equal to their ranks in Riding to a single check (using any skill) they make while mounted on beast or vehicle.
