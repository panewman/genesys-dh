# Wall Walk
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-action  
**Ranked:** No  
Suffer 2 strain and make an Average ( #GenesysChallenge #GenesysDifficulty ) Telekinesis check. If successful, you gain the ability to walk on walls and ceilings until the beginning of the next round. While this effect is active, you also ignore any #GenesysSetback imposed by low or high gravity. You may maintain this effect using concentration.
