# Ensorcelled (Improved)
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Ensorcelled talent to benefit from this talent.  
If your character has at least two ranks in a magic skill, when using the Ensorcelled talent, they may add #GenesysSuccess #GenesysAdvantage to their next social skill check (instead of #GenesysAdvantage ).
