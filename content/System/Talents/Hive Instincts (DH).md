# Hive Instincts
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
When you purchase this talent, your character gains Stealth or Streetwise as a career skill. In addition, once per session when in a crowded environment (such as a busy city street or a bustling party), your character may use this talent to add #GenesysSetback to any Perception or Vigilance checks made to detect them until the end of the encounter, or until they leave the crowd, whichever comes first.
