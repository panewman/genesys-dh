# Teleportation (Improved)
**Tier:** 5  
**Activation:** Active-action  
**Ranked:** Yes  
Your character must have purchased the Teleportation talent to benefit from this talent. Your character may use this talent to make a Hard ( #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty ) Arcana check to create a permanent beacon, glyph, or teleportation circle in a location (how it appears is up to you and your GM, but it should be large and noticeable).  
When your character uses the Teleportation talent, if they choose to teleport to the location of their beacon, glyph, or teleportation circle, they reduce the difficulty of the check to Average ( #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty ) and do not add #GenesysSetback for teleporting additional allies. They also do not upgrade the difficulty of the check if teleporting to a different world or plane of existence.   
Your character can have two glyphs, beacons, or circles established at any one time per rank of Teleportation (Improved). If they create any over this limit, one of the originals of your character’s choice is destroyed.
