# Slayer (Supreme)
**Tier:** 4  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Slayer (Improved) talent to benefit from this talent.  
Your character adds #GenesysBoost to melee combat checks against Daemon adversaries. This effect stacks with the Slayer and Slayer (Improved) talents.
