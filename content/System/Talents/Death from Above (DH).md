# Death from Above
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
When using a jump pack to move, your character may engage a foe as part of the movement, so long as that foe is within range of the jump. Until the end of the round, any attack checks your character makes targeting this foe increase their damage by 2.
