# Resourceful Mechanic
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** Yes  
When your character makes a Tech-Use check to repair system strain or hull trauma on a vehicle, they repair one additional system strain or hull trauma per rank of Resourceful Mechanic.
