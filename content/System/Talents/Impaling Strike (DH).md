# Impaling Strike
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
When your character inflicts a Critical Injury with a melee weapon, you may use this talent to immobilize the target (in addition to the other effects of the Critical Injury). This effect lasts until the end of the target’s next turn.
