# Implacable
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** Yes  
When another character targets your character with a social skill check, your character reduces any strain they suffer as a result of that heck by 1 per rank of Implacable. Your character’s ranks in Implacable cannot exceed their Presence.
