# Strength of Faith
**Tier:** 4  
**Activation:** Active-maneuver  
**Ranked:** No  
Once per session, your character may use this talent to add #GenesysSuccess equal to their ranks in Discipline and #GenesysAdvantage equal to their ranks in Willpower to the results of the next Divine skill check they make during this turn.
