# Signature Power (Improved)
**Tier:** 4  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Only psykers may benefit from this talent.Your character must have purchased the Signature Power talent to benefit from this talent. When your character manifests their signature power, reduce the difficulty of the check by two instead of one.
