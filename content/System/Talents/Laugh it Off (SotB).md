# Laugh it Off
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-incidential-oot  
**Ranked:** No  
When your character is targeted by a social check they may use this talent to spend #GenesysThreat #GenesysThreat #GenesysThreat or #GenesysDespair to reduce any strain the check inflicts by a number equal to their ranks in Charm. If they do so, the character who targeted them suffers an amount of strain equal to the amount of strain reduced.
