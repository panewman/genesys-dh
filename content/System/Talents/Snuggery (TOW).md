# Snuggery
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character gains a hidden storage somewhere in the nearest city or a hidden compartment on the vehicle they are currently traveling by (player's choice). They may store items with a total encumbrance equal to their ranks in Streetwise for hidden compartment and five times their ranks in Streetwise for hidden storage. Once per in-game week, it may be moved to another location.
