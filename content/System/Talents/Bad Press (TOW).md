# Bad Press
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-action  
**Ranked:** No  
Once per session, your character may make a Hard ( #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty ) Knowledge (Academic) check targeting an easily distinguishable group of characters (for example members of a particular guild or servants of a nobleman). If the check is successful, during the next social encounter, decrease strain thresholds of all members of the targeted group by your character's Presence rating.
