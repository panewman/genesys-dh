# Guard Dog
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased Small, But Vicious Dog talent to benefit from this talent.  
Once per round, your character may use this talent to guide their dog to perform the Guard Dog maneuver. Until the end of your character's next turn, the targeted character within the dog's short range increases their melee defense by one.
