# Suppressing Fire
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-maneuver  
**Ranked:** Yes  
If your character does not make a combat check during their turn, they may use this talent to target one character (or minion group) within long range. The target must upgrade the difficulty of any ranged combat check they make once until the end of your character’s next turn. Your character may choose to affect one additional target for each additional rank of Suppressing Fire. Your character must be holding a ranged weapon to use this talent. Your GM can also rule that your character can’t use this talent if they have no line of fire or range to the target.
