# Rites of Banishment
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-maneuver  
**Ranked:** Yes  
Once per round your character may perform the Rites of Banishment maneuver. Until the beginning of your next turn, Daemons within medium range add #GenesysSetback to Willpower checks equal to your character’s ranks in Rites of Banishment.
