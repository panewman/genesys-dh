# Surgical Strike
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
Before your character makes a Brawl or Melee (Light) check, you may spend a Story Point to use this talent to grant your attack the Vicious item quality equal to your character's ranks in Medicine.  
Note: Originally from The Forge, episode 11.
