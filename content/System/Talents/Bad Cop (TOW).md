# Bad Cop
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** Yes  
Your character may spend #GenesysAdvantage #GenesysAdvantage from a Coercion or Deception check to use this talent to upgrade the ability of a single ally’s subsequent social skill check a number of times equal to your character's ranks 1n Bad Cop. The check must target the same character as your character’s initial check, and it must take place during the same encounter.
