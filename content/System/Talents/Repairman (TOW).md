# Repairman
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Quick Fix talent to benefit from this talent.  
When your character uses the Quick Fix talent, they may choose to remove a jam or malfunction instead of temporarily repairing the item. The item is still damaged by one step after a number of rounds equal to your character's ranks in Crafts.
