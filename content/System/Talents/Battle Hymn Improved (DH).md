# Battle Hymn (Improved)
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Battle Hymn talent to benefit from this talent. While your character is chanting their battle hymn, heretics, daemons, xenos and others who fear the Emperor’s wrath within range of its effect who can hear it add #GenesysSetback to their checks.
