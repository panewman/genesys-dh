# Crushing Blow
**Tier:** 5  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
Once per session after rolling a melee attack with a single weapon but before resolving the check, your character may suffer 4 strain to use this talent. While resolving the check, the weapon gains the Breach 1 and Knockdown item qualities, and destroys one item the target is wielding that does not have the Reinforced quality. This talent may not be used with unarmed attacks.
