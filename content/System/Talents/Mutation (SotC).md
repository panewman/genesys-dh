# Mutation
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** Yes  
This talent cost 3 Æmber plus Æmber equal to your character’s current ranks in this talent. When your character purchases a rank in Mutation, they gain one randomly generated species feature from Table 1-8: Species Features, on page 47 (SotC). If you generate a feature your character already possesses, reroll the result. Your character’s ranks in Mutation cannot exceed their ranks in Resilience.
