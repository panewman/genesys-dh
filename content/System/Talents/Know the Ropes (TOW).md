# Know the Ropes
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-incidential-oot  
**Ranked:** No  
Once per encounter, before one of your character's allies makes a check, you may spend one Story Point to use this talent. Your character adds a number of #GenesysSuccess equal to their ranks in Sailing to the results of the check. When your character uses this talent, you should explain how their mastery of Sailing helped the other character accomplish their task.
