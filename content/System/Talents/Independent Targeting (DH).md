# Independent Targeting
**Tier:** 4  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
After a successful combined check with two Ranged (Light) weapons, additional hits can be allocated to other targets within range of the weapon. In order to use this talent, your initial target must be the opponent who would be most difficult to hit.
