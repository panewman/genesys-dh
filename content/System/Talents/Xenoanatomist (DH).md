# Xeno-anatomist
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Specialist Knowledge talent to benefit from this talent. Your attacks reduce their critical rating by 1 (to a minimum of 1) against xenos creatures for which you have a relevant Specialist Knowledge talent.
