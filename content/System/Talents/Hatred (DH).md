# Hatred
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** Yes  
When you gain this talent, select a target for your character’s hatred. This must be a specific group, such as gangers, daemons or psykers. When making attacks against a target that belongs to this group, add #GenesysBoost to the roll. To retreat or surrender when fighting a foe that belongs to this group, your character must first pass an Average ( #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty ) Cool check. Each additional time you purchase this talent for your character, select two additional targets for your character’s hatred.
