# Scathing Tirade
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Active-action  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character may use this talent to make an Average ( #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty ) Coercion check. For each #GenesysSuccess the check generates, one enemy within short range suffers 1 strain. For each #GenesysAdvantage , one enemy affected by Scathing Tirade suffers 1 additional strain.
