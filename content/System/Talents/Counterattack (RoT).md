# Counterattack
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-incidential-oot  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Improved Parry talent to benefit from this talent. When your character uses the Improved Parry talent to hit an attacker, they may also activate an item quality of the weapon they used as if they had generated #GenesysAdvantage #GenesysAdvantage on a combat check using that weapon.
