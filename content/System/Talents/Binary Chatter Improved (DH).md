# Binary Chatter (Improved)
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Binary Chatter talent to benefit from this talent. When your character directs a servitor to assist any character with a check, that servitor provides an additional #GenesysBoost to the check for which it is assisting (beyond the usual benefits of assistance).
