# Rule of Chamon (Supreme)
**Tier:** 5  
**Activation:** Active-incidential-oot  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Rule of Chamon (Improved) talent to benefit from this talent.  
At the beginning of an encounter, your character may spend a Story Point to perform an Augment or Barrier magic action as an out of turn incidental.  
Purchasing this talent changes your character's appearance even further, as their skin turns golden and their voice sounds metallic.
