# Inventor
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** Yes  
When your character makes a check to construct new items or modify existing ones, use this talent to add a number of #GenesysBoost to the check equal to ranks of Inventor. In addition, your character may attempt to reconstruct devices that they have heard described but have not seen and do not have any kinds of plans or schematics for.
