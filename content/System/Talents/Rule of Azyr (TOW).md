# Rule of Azyr
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Whenever your character casts an Attack spell, they must always add the Lightning effect to the spell without increasing the difficulty.  
Purchasing this talent also has a narrative effect as it subtly changes appearance of your character. Blue flecks appear in their eyes and some hair may go gray as the wind of Azyr flows through their body.
