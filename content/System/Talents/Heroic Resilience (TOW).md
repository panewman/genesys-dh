# Heroic Resilience
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-incidential-oot  
**Ranked:** No  
Once per encounter, when your character suffers wounds, you may spend one Story Point to use this talent to reduce wounds suffered by a number equal to your character's ranks in Resilience.
