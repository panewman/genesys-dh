# Manipulative
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
When you purchase this talent, your character gains Deception or Skulduggery as a career skill. In addition, once per session your character may use this talent to add #GenesysBoost to a skill check to create or maintain a disguise, fake identity, or similar cover.
