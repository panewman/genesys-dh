# Battle Hymn
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Active-maneuver  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character may use this talent to begin or continue chanting devotional hymns to The Emperor, or some similar inspiring tune. While your character is doing this, allies within short range who can hear the hymn add #GenesysBoost to checks they make. The hymn and its effects end immediately if your character does not use this talent during their turn. Once the hymn ends, your character cannot use this talent again for the rest of the encounter.
