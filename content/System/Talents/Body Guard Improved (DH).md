# Body Guard (Improved)
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-incidential-oot  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Body Guard talent to benefit from this talent. Once per session, when an ally protected by your Body Guard maneuver would be hit by an attack, you may choose to suffer that hit instead of the ally.
