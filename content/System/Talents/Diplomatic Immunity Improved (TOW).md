# Diplomatic Immunity (Improved)
**Tier:** 4  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Diplomatic Immunity talent to benefit from this talent.  
When your character uses the Diplomatic Immunity talent, they may choose to expand its effect to all allies with a social status of Middle Class or Elite who are accused of committing the same crime.
