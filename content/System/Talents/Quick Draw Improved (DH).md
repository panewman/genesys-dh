# Quick Draw (Improved)
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Quick Draw Talent to benefit from this talent. You may use Quick Draw twice per round. This also allows you to reduce the prepare rating of a weapon by 2, to a minimum of 1.
