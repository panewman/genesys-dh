# Grenadier
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
When your character makes a ranged combat check with a weapon that has the Blast item quality, you may spend one Story Point to use this talent to trigger the weapon’s Blast quality, instead of spending #GenesysAdvantage (even if the attack misses). In addition, your character treats grenades as having a range of medium.
