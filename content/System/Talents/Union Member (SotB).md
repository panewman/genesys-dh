# Union Member
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character gains Athletics, Mechanics, or Operating (your choice) as a career skill. In addition, once per session, your character may collect a small favor from a member of Humanity Labor or Human First, even if they do not owe your character a favor.
