# Booming Chant
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Active-maneuver  
**Ranked:** Yes  
Your character must have purchased the Battle Hymn talent to benefit from this talent. Your character increases the range of Battle Hymn and Liturgies of Battle by one range band per rank of Booming Chant. Narratively, you should explain how your character’s voice has been amplified over such distances, typically through use of vocal implants or a simple laud hailer.
