# Field Amputation
**Tier:** 5  
**Activation:** Active-action  
**Ranked:** No  
Once per session, you may spend a Story Point to use this talent to have your character make a Formidable ( #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty ) Medicine check targeting an engaged character who has died within the last round. If the check is successful, the target character is saved, returning to life at one wound below double their wound threshold, but one of their limbs is permanently lost.
