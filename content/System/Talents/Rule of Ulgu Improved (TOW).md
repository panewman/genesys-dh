# Rule of Ulgu (Improved)
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Rule of Ulgu talent to benefit from this talent.  
Your character adds #GenesysBoost to Stealth checks, but adds #GenesysSetback to Leadership checks.  
Additionally, after your character successfully casts a Mask spell, you may spend one Story Point to use this talent to make the illusion last until the end of the encounter without need for your character to use a concentrate maneuver to maintain the spell.  
Purchasing this talent also has a narrative effect as your character 1s surprisingly quickly forgotten by other people.
