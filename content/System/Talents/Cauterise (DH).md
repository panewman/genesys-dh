# Cauterise
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Active-action  
**Ranked:** No  
Suffer 2 strain and make an Easy ( #GenesysChallenge ) Pyromancy check, targeting anyone within medium range. For each uncancelled #GenesysSuccess on this check, you may choose to heal 1 wound in the target, however for each wound healed in this way, the target suffers 1 strain.
