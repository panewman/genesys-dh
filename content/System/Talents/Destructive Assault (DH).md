# Destructive Assault
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** Yes  
When making an attack with a melee weapon, you may use this talent to increase the weapon’s base damage by your Brawn plus ranks in Destructive Assault. The weapon immediately suffers one level of damage (see Genesys Core Rulebook, page 89) after the attack is resolved.
