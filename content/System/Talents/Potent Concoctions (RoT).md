# Potent Concoctions
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
When your character makes an Alchemy check that generates #GenesysTriumph , roll an additional #GenesysProficiency and add its results to the pool, in addition to spending the #GenesysTriumph normally. When your character makes an Alchemy check that generates #GenesysDespair , roll an additional #GenesysChallenge and add its results to the pool, in addition to spending the #GenesysDespair normally. Each of these effects can occur only once per check.
