# Storm of Iron
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
When your character targets an engaged foe with a Ranged (Light) or Ranged (Heavy) weapon, they reduce the difficulty of the attack check once if they spent a maneuver to engage with the target this turn.
