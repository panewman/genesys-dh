# Researcher
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Active-action  
**Ranked:** Yes  
Your character may perform a knowledge skill check as an action to gain information about a target or subject relevant to the current encounter. If successful, your character and their allies gain an automatic a per rank of Researcher to checks they make to act on that knowledge until the end of the following round.
