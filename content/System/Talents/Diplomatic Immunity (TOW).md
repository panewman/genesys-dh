# Diplomatic Immunity
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
When your character is accused of committing a crime (except for the most serious ones like murder, worshiping Chaos etc.) you may spend one Story Point to use this talent. Your character avoids any possible fines or jail time and is released immediately, even if they were caught red-handed. However, if your character 1s, in fact, guilty, your GM may rule that this talent might only be activated by them suffering one corruption instead of spending a Story Point.
