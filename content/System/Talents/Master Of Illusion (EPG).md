# Master Of Illusion
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
After your character makes a successful Mask spell check to create an illusion, you may spend a Story Point to have your character use this talent to make the illusion last until the end of the encounter, without performing concentrate maneuvers, and not need to remain within the spell’s range after being cast. You may spend two Story Points to make the illusion last until the end of the session instead.
