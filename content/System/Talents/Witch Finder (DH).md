# Witch Finder
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** Yes  
You can use Psyniscience (see page 78) even if your character has no psychic abilities. Any check made to detect, track or otherwise locate psykers adds #GenesysBoost per rank of Witch Finder.
