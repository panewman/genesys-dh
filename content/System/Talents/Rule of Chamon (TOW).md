# Rule of Chamon
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Whenever your character casts an Attack spell, they must always add the Impact effect to the spell without increasing the difficulty.  
Purchasing this talent also has a narrative effect as it subtly changes the appearance of your character. Their skin becomes slightly yellowish and shiny and their gaze seems to be more focused.
