# Templar (Improved)
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Templar talent to benefit from this talent. When your character casts the single Divine spell per encounter granted by the Templar talent, they do not add #GenesysSetback for wearing heavy armor (armor with +2 soak or higher), using a shield, or not having at least one hand free (see Table III.2–3: Penalties When Casting Spells, on page 210 of the Genesys Core Rulebook).
