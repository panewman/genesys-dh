# Street Slang
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** Yes  
When your character makes a social skill check in criminal or dubious company (as determined by your GM), they may suffer a number of strain to use this talent to add an equal number of #GenesysAdvantage to the check. The number may not exceed your character's ranks in Street Slang.
