# Net Search
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Active-maneuver  
**Ranked:** No  
If your character has access to the Network, they may use this talent to upgrade the ability of the next Knowledge check they make during their turn twice and the difficulty of that check once. Your GM must spend a #GenesysDespair to have your character learn  some seemingly relevant and believable information that turns out to be completely (and possibly maliciously) false.
