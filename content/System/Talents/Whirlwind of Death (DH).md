# Whirlwind of Death
**Tier:** 4  
**Activation:** Active-action  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character may perform the Whirlwind of Death action, making a melee combat check against one engaged target, increasing the difficulty of the check by one. You may spend aa generated on this combat check to hit one additional target your character is engaged with. You may do this once per engaged target, paying aa for each additional hit. When performing the Whirlwind of Death action, your character must always target the opponent with the highest difficulty and highest defense (if two targets have the same difficulty and defense, the GM chooses which target is the initial target).
