# Rule of Azyr (Supreme)
**Tier:** 5  
**Activation:** Active-incidential-oot  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Rule of Azyr (Improved) talent to benefit from this talent.  
At the beginning of an encounter, your character may spend a Story Point to perform a Predict magic action as an out of turn incidental.  
Purchasing this talent changes your characters' appearance even further, as their eyes turn completely blue and hair silver-gray. Immense power, but also calmness emanates from your character.
