# Two-Handed Stance
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** Yes  
When performing a combat check with a Ranged (Light) or Melee (Light) weapon, if your character has nothing in their other hand, they add #GenesysAdvantage to the results.
