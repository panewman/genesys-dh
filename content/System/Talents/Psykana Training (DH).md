# Psykana Training
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Active-action  
**Ranked:** No  
Only Psykers may benefit from this talent. When you purchase this talent, your character gains any psychic discipline (your choice) as a career skill. In addition, once per session your character may manifest a utility psychic power using a psychic discipline which they have no ranks in.
