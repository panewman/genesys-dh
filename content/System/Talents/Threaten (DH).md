# Threaten
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Active-incidential-oot  
**Ranked:** Yes  
After an adversary within short range of your character resolves a combat check that deals damage to one of your character’s allies, your character may suffer 3 strain to inflict a number of strain on the adversary equal to your character’s ranks in Coercion. The range of this talent increases by one band per rank of Threaten beyond the first.
