# Share Pain
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-incidential-oot  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Small, But Vicious Dog or Animal Companion talent to benefit from this talent.  
When your character's animal companion suffers wounds, your character may use this talent to reduce the wounds by half (rounded up), then your character suffers those wounds instead.
