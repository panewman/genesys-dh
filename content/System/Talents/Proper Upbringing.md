# Proper Upbringing
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** Yes  
When your character makes a social skill check in polite company (as determined by your GM), they may suffer a number of strain to use this talent to add an equal number of #GenesysAdvantage to the check. The number may not exceed your character’s ranks in Proper Upbringing.
