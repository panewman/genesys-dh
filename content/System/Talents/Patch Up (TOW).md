# Patch Up
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
When your character fails a check to heal wounds, they may instead heal a number of wounds equal to their Intellect. Once before the end of the session, #GenesysThreat #GenesysThreat or #GenesysDespair may be spent from a check the healed character makes to have them suffer a number of wounds equal to your character’s Intellect. Your character may only use this talent on a particular target once per game session.
