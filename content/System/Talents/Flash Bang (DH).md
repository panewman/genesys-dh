# Flash Bang
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Active-maneuver  
**Ranked:** No  
You create a bright flash of light and a deafening bang. Suffer 2 strain and make an Average ( #GenesysChallenge #GenesysDifficulty ) Pyromancy check. If successful, every creature within Short range (not including you) is disoriented for one round. #GenesysTriumph may be spent to extend this range to Medium, and each a may be spent to spare a single ally from the effects of the power.
