# Call Item
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Active-maneuver  
**Ranked:** No  
When you use this talent, make an Easy ( #GenesysChallenge ) Telekinesis check. If successful you summon a pre-prepared item to your hand, from anywhere within the same solar system. The item may be summoned from anywhere within the same sector with an Average ( #GenesysChallenge #GenesysDifficulty ) Telekinesis check. To prepare the object, you must spend one hour in deep meditation, infusing the object with your psychic imprint, and marking it with glyphs and runes. You may only have one prepared item at a time, and this item must have an Encumbrance rating of 3 or less.
