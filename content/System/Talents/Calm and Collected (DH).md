# Calm and Collected
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
When you purchase this talent, your character gains Cool or Discipline as a career skill. In addition, once per session your character may spend #GenesysTriumph on a successful skill check to negate the ongoing negative effects of a fear check. This talent can’t be used to negate mental traumas.
