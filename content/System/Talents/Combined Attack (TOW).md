# Combined Attack
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
Once per round, when your character makes a melee combat check, they may suffer 3 strain to use this talent. Upgrade the difficulty of the check once. While resolving the check, the weapon gains the Linked item quality with a rating equal to your character's ranks in Coordination.
