# Death or Glory
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-incidential-oot  
**Ranked:** No  
When your character would be called on to make a fear check due to encountering a foe with the fear trait within short range, you may spend a story point to immediately engage the foe and perform a single melee attack, before performing a fear check. If the foe is slain by your attack, no fear check is necessary, otherwise, resolve the fear check as normal.
