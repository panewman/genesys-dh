# Know Thy Enemy
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Specialist Knowledge talent to benefit from this talent. Your character reduces the difficulty of fear checks by 1 if they are caused by a group for which you have a relevant Specialist Knowledge talent. This talent provides no benefit to fear checks caused by warp entities or daemons.
