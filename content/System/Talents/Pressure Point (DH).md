# Pressure Point
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
When your character makes an unarmed Melee (Light) check targeting a living opponent, they may declare it as a Pressure Point attack before rolling. If they do so, the attack deals strain damage instead of wound damage, and inflicts additional strain damage equal to their ranks in Medicae.
