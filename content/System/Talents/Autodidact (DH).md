# Autodidact
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
When you purchase this talent, your character gains Common Lore, Forbidden Lore or Scholastic Lore as a career skill. In addition, if your character spends at least one hour engaged in dedicated research or study as part of a Knowledge skill check, they recover 3 strain.
