# Fearsome
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** Yes  
When an adversary becomes engaged with your character, you may force them to make a fear check, with difficulty equal to your character’s ranks in Fearsome. At the GM’s discretion, some adversaries may be immune to this talent based on the type of adversary or the ongoing circumstances.
