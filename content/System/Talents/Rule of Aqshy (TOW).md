# Rule of Aqshy
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Whenever your character casts an Attack spell, they must always add the Fire effect to the spell without increasing the difficulty.  
Purchasing this talent also have a narrative effect as it subtly changes the appearance of your character. Red strands appear among the character's hair, and their face blushes slightly.
