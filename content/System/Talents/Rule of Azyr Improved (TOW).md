# Rule of Azyr (Improved)
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Rule of Azyr talent to benefit from this talent.  
Your character cannot be disoriented and gains #GenesysBoost to all Negotiation checks. Additionally, once per encounter, your character may spend a Story Point to fly using the hovering guidelines in the Flying sidebar on page 100 of the Genesys Core Rulebook. This effect remains until the end of the encounter.  
Purchasing this talent further changes your character's appearance, as their eyes become blue with a faraway look and subtle chill wind moves their clothes.
