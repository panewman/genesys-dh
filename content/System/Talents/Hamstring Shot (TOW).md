# Hamstring Shot
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Active-action  
**Ranked:** No  
Once per encounter, your character may use this talent to perform a ranged combat check against one non-vehicle target within range of the weapon used. If the check is successful, halve the damage inflicted by the attack (before reducing damage by the target’s soak). The target is immobilized until the end of its next turn. Note: Balance change for the original Hamstring Shot talent.
