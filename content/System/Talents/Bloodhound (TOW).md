# Bloodhound
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
When your character makes a check to follow another character’s trail (whether their physical tracks or a trail of information), you may use this talent to add a number of #GenesysAdvantage equal to your character’s ranks in Streetwise or Survival, as appropriate for the environment, to the check results.
