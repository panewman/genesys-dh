# Snare
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-maneuver  
**Ranked:** No  
Once per encounter, your character may spend a maneuver to use this talent to set a trap. Once before the end of the encounter, you may spend a Story Point to force one character in the encounter to make an opposed Vigilance vs your character's Survival or Streetwise check as an out of turn incidental. If they fail, they are staggered until the end of their next turn, plus one additional turn per #GenesysThreat #GenesysThreat .
