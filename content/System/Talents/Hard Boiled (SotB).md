# Hard Boiled
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
When your character makes a check to recover strain at the end of an encounter (as describe on page 117 of the Genesys Core Rulebook), your character may make a Simple (-) Resilience check instead of Discipline or Cool. If your character does so, they heal 1 strain per #GenesysSuccess and 1 wound per #GenesysAdvantage .
