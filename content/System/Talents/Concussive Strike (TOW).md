# Concussive Strike
**Tier:** 5  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
Once per round, before performing a Melee (Light) or Melee (Heavy) check, your character may suffer two strain to use this talent. Increase the difficulty of the check by one; while resolving the check the weapon gains the Concussive 1 quality.
