# Dig In
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-maneuver  
**Ranked:** No  
As a maneuver, your character may shout orders to a number of allied characters within long range equal to your character’s ranks in Leadership. Until the start of your character’s next turn, your character and all targeted allies increase the ranged defence provided by any cover they are using by 1.
