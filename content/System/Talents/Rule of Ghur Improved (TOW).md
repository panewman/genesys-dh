# Rule of Ghur (Improved)
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Rule of Ghur talent to benefit from this talent.  
Your character increases their melee defense by one, but upgrades the difficulty of Charm and Negotiation checks once. Additionally, when your character casts a Conjure spell, they always add the Summon Ally effect without increasing the difficulty, and they must always summon a living animal. The animal remains for the duration of the encounter without need for your character to use a concentrate maneuver to maintain the spell.  
Purchasing this talent further changes your character's appearance, as they appear more bestial and speak with a harsh voice.
