# Purity of Hatred
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Hatred talent to benefit from this talent. When you gain this talent, select a target group for which your character possesses the Hatred talent. When making attacks against a target that belongs to this group, your attacks gain Vicious 2, or increase their existing Vicious quality by 2. You may also spend a Story Point to reduce the attack’s critical rating by 1 (to a minimum of 1). You may purchase this talent multiple times, selecting a new target group each time.
