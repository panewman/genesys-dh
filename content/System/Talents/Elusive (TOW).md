# Elusive
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-incidential-oot  
**Ranked:** No  
When another character makes a check to follow your character’s trail (whether physical tracks or a trail of information), your character may use this talent to add a number of #GenesysThreat equal to their ranks in Streetwise or Survival, as appropriate to the environment, to the check results.
