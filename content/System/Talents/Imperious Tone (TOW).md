# Imperious Tone
**Tier:** 4  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
When your character makes a Charm or Coercion check targeting a character of a lower social status than they are, they may suffer 2 strain to use this talent. Count your character's ranks of Charm or Coercion as equal to their ranks in Leadership.
