# Corpus Conversion
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** Yes  
Only Psykers may benefit from this talent. When making a psychic power check, you may suffer 2 wounds per rank of Corpus Conversion. For each 2 wounds suffered in this way, add #GenesysSuccess or #GenesysAdvantage #GenesysAdvantage to the results of the check.
