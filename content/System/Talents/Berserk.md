# Berserk
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Active-maneuver  
**Ranked:** No  
Once per encounter, your character may use this talent. Until the end of the encounter or until they are incapacitated, your character adds #GenesysSuccess #GenesysAdvantage #GenesysAdvantage to all melee combat checks they make. However, opponents add #GenesysSuccess to all combat checks targeting your character. While berserk, your character cannot make ranged combat checks. At the end of the encounter (or when they are incapacitated), your character suffers 6 strain.
