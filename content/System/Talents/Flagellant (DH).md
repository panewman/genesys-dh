# Flagellant
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** Yes  
If your character spends ten minutes or more ritually inflicting pain on themselves, they may reduce their wound threshold by 1 a number of times equal to their ranks in Flagellant. For each point their wound threshold is reduced in this way, they add #GenesysBoost to all Corruption checks and checks to resist mind altering psychic/warp effects. These effects last until the beginning of the next day.
