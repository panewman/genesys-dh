# Wall of Steel
**Tier:** 4  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Parry talent to benefit from this talent. When an ally engaged with your character suffers a hit from a melee combat check, your character may use a Parry incidental to reduce the damage the hit deals (even though you are not the target of the combat check).
