# Man's Best Friend
**Tier:** 5  
**Activation:** Active-incidential-oot  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Small, But Vicious Dog talent to benefit from this talent.  
Once per session, immediately after suffering a Critical Injury, your character may suffer one corruption to use this talent to guide their dog to perform an Average ( #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty ) Coordination check as an out of turn incidental. If the check is successful, your character does not suffer the Critical Injury, but their dog perishes.
