# Gate of Infinity
**Tier:** 4  
**Activation:** Active-action  
**Ranked:** No  
Once per session, suffer 4 strain and make a Hard ( #GenesysChallenge #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty ) Telekinesis check, choosing a target location within as many kilometres as two times your ranks in Discipline. You must be aware of the location chosen, either having visited it before, seen it from afar, or simply studied it on a map. If successful, a hole in space is torn open within short range. This hole is a gate, linking directly to the target location. Any persons or objects of Silhouette 1 or lower may move through it freely. Each #GenesysAdvantage #GenesysAdvantage may increase the possible Silhouette by 1, to a maximum of 4. The gate remains open for one round per #GenesysSuccess . Any individual who passes through the gate must make a Hard ( #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty ) corruption check at the end of the encounter.
