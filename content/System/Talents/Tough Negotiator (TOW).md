# Tough Negotiator
**Tier:** 4  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
When your character inflicts a critical remark (See page 123 of the Genesys Core Rulebook) in a Negotiation check, they inflict additional strain to the target equal to your character's ranks in Negotiation.
