# Master of Shadows
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
When your character successfully hides from someone by performing a Stealth check, they may use this talent to automatically hide one additional character in engaged range for each #GenesysSuccess except the first. The number of additional characters hidden cannot exceed your character's ranks in Stealth.
