# Trick of the Light
**Tier:** 5  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
When making a combat check with a laser or maser weapon, your character may use this talent to spend #GenesysAdvantage to inflict one additional hit with this weapon, dealing base damage plus damage equal to the total number of #GenesysSuccess scored on the check. This hit may target the original target or another target within short range of the original target.
