# Coordinated Strike
**Tier:** 5  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Coordinated Assault talent to benefit from this talent.  
When your character uses the Coordinated Assault talent, in addition to the standard effect of the talent, your character may suffer 4 strain to use this talent. Allies affected by Coordinated Assault add damage equal to your character's ranks in Coordinated Assault to all combat checks they make until the end of your character’s next turn.
