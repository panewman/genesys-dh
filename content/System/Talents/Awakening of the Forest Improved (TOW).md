# Awakening of the Forest (Improved)
**Tier:** 5  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Awakening of the Forest talent to benefit from this talent.  
When using Awakening of the Forest, use the Forest Guardian characteristic (Realms of Terrinoth, page 179) for the awakened tree, instead of Aemhelin Scion.
