# One of Us!
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** Yes  
Select one organization from Chapter 2: Organizations (or create a new one with your GM’s permission). Your character becomes a known and devout follower of that organization, and adds #GenesysAdvantage per rank of One of Us! to social skill checks they make targeting its members. If your character is discovered acting in a manner antithetical to the organization’s interest, though, your GM may instead add #GenesysThreat or other penalties to such checks.
