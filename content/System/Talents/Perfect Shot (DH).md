# Perfect Shot
**Tier:** 5  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
When attacking with a ranged weapon, if your attack successfully incapacitates your target, you may spend #GenesysTriumph to immediately perform an attack against another target within range as an incidental. Further attacks may not be triggered by #GenesysTriumph generated on this incidental attack.
