# Overcharge (Improved)
**Tier:** 5  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Overcharge talent to benefit from this talent. When using the Overcharge talent, your character may spend #GenesysAdvantage #GenesysAdvantage or #GenesysTriumph from the Mechanics check to immediately take one additional action. This talent can only be used once per check.
