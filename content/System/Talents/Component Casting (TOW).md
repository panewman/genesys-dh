# Component Casting
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
When your character casts a spell, they may use this talent to consume a physical item and add j to the check. The item's price and rarity depend on the spell's power and effect, and the item has to be thematically appropriate for the spell. For example, casting a fireball may require a bit of sulfur, while transforming into a wolf may require a ball of the animal's hair.
