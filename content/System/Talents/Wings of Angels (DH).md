# Wings of Angels
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-maneuver  
**Ranked:** Yes  
When equipped with a jump pack, your character may jump to anywhere within medium range of their current position as a maneuver, without performing a Driving check. The GM may rule that a Driving check is required if the terrain is particularly difficult to jump around in, such as a thick jungle canopy or a low ceiling.
