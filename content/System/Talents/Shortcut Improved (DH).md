# Shortcut (Improved)
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Shortcut talent to benefit from this talent. When engaging in a chase or race, your character may suffer 2 strain to add #GenesysSuccess equal to your ranks in Shortcut to the check.
