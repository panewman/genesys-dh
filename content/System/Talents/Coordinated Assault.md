# Coordinated Assault
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Active-maneuver  
**Ranked:** Yes  
Once per turn, your character may use this talent to have a number of allies engaged with your character equal to your ranks in Leadership add #GenesysAdvantage to all combat checks they make until the end of your character’s next turn. The range of this talent increases by one band per rank of Coordinated Assault beyond the first.
