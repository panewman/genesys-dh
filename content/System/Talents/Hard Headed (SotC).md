# Hard Headed
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Active-action  
**Ranked:** Yes  
If your character is staggered, they may use this talent, even though a character is normally unable to perform any actions when they are staggered. Your character makes a Daunting ( #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty ) Resilience check. If they succeed, they are no longer staggered. The difficulty of this check decreases by one per additional rank of Hard Headed, to a minimum of Easy ( #GenesysDifficulty ).
