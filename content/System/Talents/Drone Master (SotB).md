# Drone Master
**Tier:** 5  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character may control two drones or minion groups of drones no larger than your character’s Willpower (either via the rules found on page 233 of Android: Shadow of the Beanstalk or via the Animal Companion talent, found on page 77 of the Genesys Core Rulebook). Your character resolves each drone’s (or minion group’s) turn individually, choosing the order in which they activate.
