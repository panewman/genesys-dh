# Instrument of His Will
**Tier:** 4  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
After making a successful attack against a Daemon, you may spend a Story Point to inflict a number of wounds on the Daemon equal to your Willpower. This extra damage bypasses soak, including soak provided by the Daemonic trait.
