# Stromfels' Wrath
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
Once per round on your character’s turn, your character may suffer 3 strain to use this talent to add damage equal to their ranks in Sailing to one hit of a successful melee attack.
