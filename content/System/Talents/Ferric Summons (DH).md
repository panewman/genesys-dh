# Ferric Summons
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have Ferric Lure Implants installed to benefit from this talent. When using Ferric Lure Implants to summon a metal object to hand, you can affect items up to medium range away.
