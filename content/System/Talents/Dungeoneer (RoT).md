# Dungeoneer
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** Yes  
After your character makes a Perception, Vigilance, or Knowledge (Adventuring) check to notice, identify, or avoid a threat in a cavern, subterranean ruin, or similar location, your character cancels a number of uncanceled #GenesysThreat no greater than your character’s ranks in Dugeoneer.
