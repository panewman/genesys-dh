# Strike to Stun
**Tier:** 4  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Backstab or the Sucker Punch talent to benefit from this talent.  
When your character uses either the Backstab or the Sucker Punch talent, in addition to the standard effect of the talent, they may suffer 3 strain to use this talent. While resolving the check, their attack gains the Concussive 1 quality.
