# Acrobatics
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Active-maneuver  
**Ranked:** No  
When your character is moving in a way that would normally require Athletics or Coordination check (e.g. climbing, swinging a rope etc.), they may suffer 2 strain to perform this check as a maneuver instead of an action.
