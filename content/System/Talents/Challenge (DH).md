# Challenge
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Active-maneuver  
**Ranked:** Yes  
Once per encounter, your character may use this talent to choose a number of adversaries within short range no greater than your character’s ranks in Challenge (a minion group counts as a single adversary for this purpose). Until the encounter ends or your character is incapacitated, these adversaries add #GenesysBoost to combat checks targeting your character and #GenesysSetback #GenesysSetback to combat checks targeting other characters.
