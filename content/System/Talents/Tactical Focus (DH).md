# Tactical Focus
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
When performing a combat check with a Ranged (Heavy) weapon, if your character did not perform a maneuver to ready or stow a weapon or item during this turn, they add #GenesysAdvantage to the results.
