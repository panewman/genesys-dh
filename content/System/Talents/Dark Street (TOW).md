# Dark Street
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** Yes  
After your character makes a Perception, Vigilance, Streetwise, or Knowledge check to notice, identify, or avoid a threat in sewers, underground city tunnels, or other similar locations, your character cancels a number of uncanceled #GenesysThreat no greater than your character's ranks in Dark Street.
