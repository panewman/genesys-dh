# Elementalist
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
When your character gains this talent, they must choose one of the following attack spell effects (and the element associated with it): Fire (fire), Ice (water), Impact (earth), or Lightning (air). Whenever your character casts an Attack spell, they always add the chosen effect to the spell without increasing the difficulty. However, they may never add any of the other three effects to a spell they cast.
