# Net Warrior
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-action  
**Ranked:** No  
While accessing a system using a brain-machine interface (BMI), your character may use this talent to make an opposed Computers (Hacking) versus Computer (Sysops) check targeting one other character on the system that they are aware of. The target suffers 1 strain per #GenesysSuccess , and if they are using a BMI, they also suffer 1 wound per #GenesysSuccess .
