# Civilised
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
When you purchase this talent, your character gains Charm, Leadership or Negotiation as a career skill. In addition, once per session you may use this talent to ignore any penalties imposed on a social check due to differences in culture, class or other societal factors.
