# Laugh it Off
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-incidential-oot  
**Ranked:** Yes  
When your character is targeted by a social skill check, they may spend #GenesysThreat #GenesysThreat #GenesysThreat or #GenesysDespair to reduce any strain the check inflicts by a number equal to their ranks in Charm. If they do so, the character who targeted them suffers that much strain instead.
