# Encouraging Song (Improved)
**Tier:** 5  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Encouraging Song talent to benefit from this talent.  
When your character uses the Encouraging Song talent, you may spend #GenesysTriumph to decrease the difficulty of the next check of affected allies by one.
