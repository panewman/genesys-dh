# Recon
**Tier:** 4  
**Activation:** Active-action  
**Ranked:** No  
To use this talent, your character must spend some time (determined by the GM) observing a selected, easy to distinguish area. After that time, your character may make a Survival or Streetwise check, as appropriate to the environment. The base difficulty of the check is Hard ( #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty ), however it should be increased if the area 1s large, secured in any way or full of hostile inhabitants.  
If the check is successful, until the end of the game session, your character or one of their allies may add a number of #GenesysSuccess equal to your character's rank in the skill used to one check made in the area. For each #GenesysTriumph this ability may be used one additional time.
