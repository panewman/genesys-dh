# Good Cop
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** Yes  
Your character may spend #GenesysAdvantage #GenesysAdvantage during a Charm or Negotiation check to upgrade the ability of a single ally’s subsequent social skill check against the same target in the same encounter. Upgrade the ability a number of times equal to your character’s ranks in Good Cop. A single check may only benefit from one use of Good Cop.
