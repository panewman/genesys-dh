# Bad Cop
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** Yes  
Your character may spend #GenesysAdvantage #GenesysAdvantage from a Coercion or Deception check to upgrade the ability of a single ally’s subsequent Social Interaction skill check against the same target in the same encounter. Upgrade the ability a number of times equal to your character’s ranks in Bad Cop. A single check may only benefit from one use of Bad Cop.
