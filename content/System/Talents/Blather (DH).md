# Blather
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Active-action  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character may perform the Blather action against a target, making an opposed Charm or Deception check. If the check is successful, while the target is engaged in conversation with your character they add #GenesysFailure #GenesysFailure to Perception or Vigilance checks made to detect the actions of your allies. #GenesysAdvantage #GenesysAdvantage may be spent to affect an additional target.
