# Knockout Punch
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character adds the Stun quality to their Brawl combat checks with a rating equal to two plus your character’s ranks in Coordination (this does not stack with other instances of the Stun quality).
