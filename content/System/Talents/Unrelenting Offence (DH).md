# Unrelenting Offence
**Tier:** 4  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
Once per round after resolving a successful Melee (Light) or Melee (Heavy) combat check, your character may suffer 4 strain to use this talent to immediately make an additional melee attack as an incidental against the same target. Increase the difficulty of this additional combat check by #GenesysDifficulty if this attack uses a second weapon, or by #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty if the attack uses the same weapon.
