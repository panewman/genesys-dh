# Encouraging Song
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Active-action  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character may use this talent to make an Average ( #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty ) Charm check. For each #GenesysSuccess the check generates, one ally within medium range adds #GenesysBoost to their next skill check. For each #GenesysAdvantage , one ally benefiting from Encouraging Song heals 1 strain.
