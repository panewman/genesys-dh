# You Saw Nothing
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Active-action  
**Ranked:** Yes  
Your character may make a Hard ( #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty ) Coercion check to perform the You Saw Nothing action. If you succeed, you convince one uninvolved bystander NPC per rank of You Saw Nothing to leave the scene without asking questions. If interrogated about the matter later, these NPCs deny having seen anything or have forgotten anything of importance.
