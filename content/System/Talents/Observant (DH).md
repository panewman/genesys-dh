# Observant
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
When you purchase this talent, your character gains Perception or Vigilance as a career skill. In addition, once per session your character may use this talent to add #GenesysSuccess to the results of an initiative check. This talent must be used before any dice are rolled.
