# Craft Specialization
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** Yes  
For each rank of this talent, select a specific area of craftsmanship for your character. When your character makes a Crafts check which pertains to that area of craftsmanship, reduce the difficulty of the check once. If the check is to craft an item (see Realms of Terrinoth, page 112), additionally decrease the time needed by half (rounded up). When your character makes a Crafts check which DOES NOT pertain to that area of craftsmanship, add #GenesysSetback #GenesysSetback to the check instead.
