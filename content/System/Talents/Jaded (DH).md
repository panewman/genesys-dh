# Jaded
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-incidential-oot  
**Ranked:** No  
When prompted to make a fear check, your character may suffer a number of strain equal to the difficulty of the fear check to ignore the fear check entirely (this must be done before any rolls are made). This talent may not be used to ignore fear checks imposed by Daemons, Warp manifestations or other unholy, Warp tainted events.
