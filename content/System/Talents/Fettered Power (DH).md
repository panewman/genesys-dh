# Fettered Power
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
Only psykers may benefit from this talent. When making a psychic power check, you may choose to ignore the effects of any #GenesysAdvantage (they still cancel out #GenesysThreat as usual). This choice is made before rolling any dice. If you do so, downgrade the difficulty pool of the check once. This may not be done at the same time as Pushing.
