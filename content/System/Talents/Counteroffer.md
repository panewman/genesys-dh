# Counteroffer
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Active-action  
**Ranked:** No  
Once per session, your character may use this talent to choose one non-nemesis adversary within medium range and make an opposed Negotiation versus Discipline check. If successful, the target becomes staggered until the end of their next turn.  
At your GM’s discretion, you may spend #GenesysTriumph on this check to have the adversary become an ally until the end of the encounter. However, the duration of this may be shortened or extended depending on whether your GM feels your offer is appealing to the adversary and whether your character follows through on their offer!
