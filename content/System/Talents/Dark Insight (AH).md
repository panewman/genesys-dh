# Dark Insight
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Active-action  
**Ranked:** No  
As long as your character has at least one rank in Knowledge (Mythos), as an action, they can make an Average ( #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty ) Knowledge (Mythos) check to detect the presence of any Mythos occurrences or objects within short range. This talent replaces the one with the same name in Realms of Terrinoth (page 84). When meeting the requisite for Blood Sacrifice, refer to the version presented here.
