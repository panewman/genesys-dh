# Body Guard (Supreme)
**Tier:** 4  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Body Guard (Improved) talent to benefit from this talent. When your character uses the Body Guard maneuver, they may protect a number of engaged characters up to their ranks in Resilience instead of just one.
