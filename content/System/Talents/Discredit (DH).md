# Discredit
**Tier:** 4  
**Activation:** Active-action  
**Ranked:** No  
Once per encounter, your character may take the Discredit action by making a Hard ( #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty ) Deception check. If you succeed, one chosen character upgrades the difficulty of all social checks once, plus one additional time for every #GenesysAdvantage #GenesysAdvantage spent, until the end of the encounter.
