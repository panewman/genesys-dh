# Signature Power
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Only psykers may benefit from this talent. When your character gains this talent, decide on a signature power for them, consisting of a particular psychic power and a specific set of one or more effects. When your character manifests their signature power (consisting of the exact combination of action and effects previously chosen), reduce the difficulty of the check by one.
