# Manann's Blessing
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
When in a nautical environment, your character may make a Simple (-) Sailing check, instead of Discipline or Cool, to recover strain at the end of an encounter (see page 117 of Genesys Core Rulebook).
