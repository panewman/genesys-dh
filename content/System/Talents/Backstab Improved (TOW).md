# Backstab (Improved)
**Tier:** 4  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Backstab talent to benefit from this talent.  
When your character uses the Backstab talent, in addition to the standard effect of the talent, they may suffer 2 strain to count the Critical rating of their weapon as one lower to a minimum of 1.
