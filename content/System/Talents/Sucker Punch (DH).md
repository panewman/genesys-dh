# Sucker Punch
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Active-incidential-oot  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character may use this talent to spend #GenesysTriumph from a check they make to determine Initiative to make an unarmed Melee (Light) combat check against a valid target before the first round of combat begins. If this combat check is successful, your character may activate the Disorient item quality without spending #GenesysAdvantage .
