# College Wizard (Supreme)
**Tier:** 5  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the College Wizard (Improved) talent to benefit from this talent.  
When your character causes a Miscast, but before determining its result, you may spend a Story Point to count the result as if it were rolled “O1”.
