# Inspiring Cry
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
Once per encounter, your character may use this talent to allow allies within medium range to reroll any number of #GenesysBoost when they make a check until the end of the round. The number of allies affected may not exceed your character's ranks in Leadership.
