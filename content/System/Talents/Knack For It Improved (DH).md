# Knack For It (Improved)
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-maneuver  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Knack For It talent to benefit from this talent. Once per round before making a skill check, you may perform the Knack For It maneuver. Your character suffers 1 strain and upgrades the ability of the skill check once. This may only be used to upgrade skills for which you have the Knack For It talent.
