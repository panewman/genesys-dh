# Dissonance
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-action  
**Ranked:** No  
While wielding a musical instrument, your character may use this talent to make an Average ( #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty ) Charm or Verse check. For each #GenesysSuccess the check generates, one enemy of the player’s choosing within medium range suffers 1 wound. For each #GenesysAdvantage , one enemy affected by Dissonance suffers 1 additional wound.
