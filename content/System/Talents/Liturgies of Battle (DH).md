# Liturgies of Battle
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-maneuver  
**Ranked:** No  
As a maneuver you may suffer 1 strain and shout rites of battle or chant Imperial war hymns. Until the start of your next turn, all allies within Short range reduce the difficulty of any Discipline and Cool checks (including fear checks) once. You may repeat this maneuver every round, suffering 1 strain each time to extend the effect.
