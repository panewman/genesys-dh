# Weapon Tech
**Tier:** 5  
**Activation:** Active-maneuver  
**Ranked:** No  
Once per encounter, your character may enhance any Melta, Plasma or Power weapon they are currently wielding. This increases the weapon’s damage by an amount equal to your ranks in Tech-Use, and also gives it Pierce with a rating equal to your character’s ranks in Tech-Use (or increases theexisting Pierce rating by the same amount). This enhancement lasts until the end of the round.
