# Æmber Enhancement
**Tier:** 5  
**Activation:** Active-maneuver  
**Ranked:** No  
This talent costs 6 Æmber. When your character purchases this talent, choose two characteristics. Once per session, your character may consume 2 Æmber to use this talent to increase one of the chosen characteristics by one until the end of the current encounter or scene. This may increase a characteristic to a maximum of six.
