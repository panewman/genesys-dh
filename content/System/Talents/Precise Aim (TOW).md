# Precise Aim
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** Yes  
When your character performs an Aim maneuver, they may suffer 2 strain to use this talent. For their next combat check, your character decreases the target’s defense by a number equal to their ranks in Precise Aim.
