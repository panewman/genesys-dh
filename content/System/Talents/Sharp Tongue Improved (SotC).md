# Sharp Tongue (Improved)
**Tier:** 5  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Sharp Tongue talent to benefit from this talent. When your character inflicts a critical remark in a social encounter, they may for the target to capitulate (see page 122 of the Core Rulebook).
