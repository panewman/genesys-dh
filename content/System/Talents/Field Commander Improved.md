# Field Commander (Improved)
**Tier:** 4  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Field Commander talent to benefit from this talent. When your character uses the Field Commander talent, your character affects a number of allies equal to twice the character’s Presence. In addition, you may spend #GenesysTriumph to allow one ally to suffer 1 strain to perform an action, instead of a maneuver.
