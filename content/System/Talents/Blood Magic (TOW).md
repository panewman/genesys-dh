# Blood Magic
**Tier:** 4  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
When your character casts a spell, they may suffer 2 wounds to use this talent to trigger up to three different qualities or spell effects. These qualities or spell effects must be ones that can be triggered by spending #GenesysAdvantage or #GenesysTriumph .
