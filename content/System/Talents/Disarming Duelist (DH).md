# Disarming Duelist
**Tier:** 4  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
When you make a successful Melee (Light) check, you may spend #GenesysTriumph or #GenesysAdvantage #GenesysAdvantage to disarm your opponent. The disarmed weapon lands anywhere within short range of the engagement (your choice).
