# Pinning Fire (Improved)
**Tier:** 4  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Pinning Fire talent to benefit from this talent. When making a Pinning Fire action, you may affect a number of targets within range equal to ranks in Pinning Fire.
