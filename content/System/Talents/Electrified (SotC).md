# Electrified
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
This talent costs 3 Æmber. Your character adds the Disorient 2 and Stun 3 item qualities to their unarmed attacks. In addition, Brawl combat checks that target your character add #GenesysThreat #GenesysThreat to their results. #GenesysDespair from a Brawl check your character makes may be spent to deplete this talent.  
Being electrified may have other narrative effects, at your GM’s discretion.
