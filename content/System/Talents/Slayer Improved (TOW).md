# Slayer (Improved)
**Tier:** 3  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Slayer talent to benefit from this talent.  
Your character adds #GenesysBoost to melee combat checks against targets that are silhouette 3 or greater. This stacks with the effect of the Slayer talent.
