# Dramatic Escape
**Tier:** 4  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
Your character must have purchased the Dramatic Entrance talent to benefit from this talent.  
Your character may use the Dramatic Entrance talent to immediately escape from the current encounter instead of entering it. If your character is targeted by a check, they may escape after the dice pool is assembled, but before is rolled.
