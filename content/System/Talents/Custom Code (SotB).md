# Custom Code
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
When your character selects this talent they choose one icebreaker or piece of ice that they own. If they choose an icebreaker, whenever they use that icebreaker to override ice, they at #GenesysAdvantage to the results. If they choose a piece of ice, whenever someone else attempts to override it, they add #GenesysThreat to the results.
