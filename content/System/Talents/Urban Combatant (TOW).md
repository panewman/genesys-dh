# Urban Combatant
**Tier:** 4  
**Activation:** Active-incidential-oot  
**Ranked:** No  
When your character is targeted by a combat check while in an urban environment, you may spend one Story Point to use this talent before the dice pool is rolled. If you do so, your character's opponent removes all #GenesysSetback added to the check, and instead adds an equal number of #GenesysFailure to the results.
