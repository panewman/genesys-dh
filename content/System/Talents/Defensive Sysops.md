# Defensive Sysops
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Passive  
**Ranked:** No  
When attempting to defend a computer system against intrusion (or when someone attempts to hack a computer owned or programmed by your character) your character adds #GenesysSetback #GenesysSetback to their opponent’s checks. If your character has access to the computer system when the intrusion takes place, they are automatically aware of the intrusion.
