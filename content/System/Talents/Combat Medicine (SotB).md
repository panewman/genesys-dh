# Combat Medicine
**Tier:** 2  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** Yes  
Before making a Medicine check, your character may use this talent to add #GenesysSuccess equal to their ranks in Combat Medicine to the results. After the check is resolved, the target suffers 2 strain for each rank your character has in Combat Medicine.
