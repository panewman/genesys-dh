# Street Fighter
**Tier:** 1  
**Activation:** Active-incidential  
**Ranked:** No  
When your character disorients or knocks their target prone while making a combat check when unarmed or using brawling weapons such as brass knuckles, they may use this talent to cause the target to suffer wounds equal to your character’s ranks in Skulduggery. Power fists do not count as a brawling weapon for this purpose.
