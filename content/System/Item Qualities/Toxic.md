# Toxic (Passive)
When this weapon damages a target and bypasses soak, they are forced to roll
a Resilience check with a difficulty equal to the weapon’s Toxic rating. If failed, each uncancelled
#GenesysFailure inflicts one wound, ignoring soak. Each #GenesysThreat #GenesysThreat inflicts one point of strain.
