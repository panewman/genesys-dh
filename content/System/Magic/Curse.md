# Narrative
**Skills:** Arcana, Divine
What we call a "Curse" is any sort of affliction that applies a negative effect to a character, whether it be a penalty to combat checks, a wasting sickness incurable by mundane means, or an inability to speak a certain name. The most extreme afflictions might even bend a victim to the spellcaster’s will. The distinction between affliction and attack might not always be clear. As a general principle, if a magical action inflicts damage or strain, it’s an attack. If it simply makes a target more susceptible to damage or strain, it’s a curse.

Outside of structured encounters, curses could take any number of forms. Some could be annoying, such as someone sneezing uncontrollably or feeling inexplicably lethargic. The effects could be represented mechanically by adding a #GenesysSetback or two to the target’s checks. The check to inflict such a minor curse should either be opposed (see page 25), or **Easy ( #GenesysDifficulty )** if the target is a minor character. Other curses could be more dangerous, such as a hex that causes dangerous things to happen to the target for no reason (ladders breaking, tree branches falling on them, and the like). Not only should the difficulty of the spell be higher— if it’s not an opposed check, it should be at least **Average ( #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty )** and possibly **Hard ( #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty )**— but the effects can be represented narratively. If a character falls from a broken ladder, for example, use the falling rules on page 112.

Of course, there are some truly terrible curses: blindness, disease, madness, or being turned into a toad. These checks should almost always be opposed (either by the target’s Discipline or Resilience, depending on whether the curse affects the mind or the body). You
can pull effects from the Table I.6–10: Critical Injury Result on page 115, the fear and sanity rules on page 243 and 244, or use similar inspiration when figuring out what the curse does.

# Structural
**Concentration:** Yes
**Skills:** Arcana, Divine
This action represents the combat use of curse magic. Your character selects one target within short range, then makes an Arcana or Divine skill check. The default difficulty of the check is **Average ( #GenesysDifficulty #GenesysDifficulty )**. If it is successful, until the end of the character’s next turn, the target decreases the ability of any skill checks they make by one (in effect, this means they remove one #GenesysAbility from their checks).
